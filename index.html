Heterogeneous Multi-Agent Learning in Isaac Lab: Scalable Simulation for Robotic Collaboration

IROS 2025, first author

Jacob Haight, Isaac Peterson, Christopher Allred, Mario Harper

Most multi-agent RL tooling assumes every agent is the same robot. I added heterogeneous-agent RL algorithms (HAPPO, HATRPO, HAA2C, alongside MAPPO baselines) to NVIDIA Isaac Lab, so teams of different robots, such as legged and wheeled platforms, can be trained together in GPU-parallel physics simulation.

Paper Code Project page Video on YouTube

The framework became a platform for the lab's follow-up work on adversarial heterogeneous teams (Peterson et al., 2025).

Strawberry Robotics

Co-founder & CTO, 2025–present

Strawberry Robotics is building SHARP, a gantry-mounted, two-armed harvesting robot for strawberries and other canopy crops. We were a two-person founding team; I owned the AI side end to end: simulation, perception, and robot learning.

What I built

Simulation environment
The Isaac Sim strawberry environment with the custom SHARP arms, used to record teleoperated picks and to train and test policies.
Perception
A point-transformer model that segments depth scans of strawberry plants into leaves and berries and predicts each berry's stem attachment point and direction, plus a YOLO detector, integrated into one pose-estimation pipeline.
Robot learning
Teleoperated demonstrations for imitation learning on a multistep picking task, fine-tuning of a large vision-language-action model on our own dataset, and an automated reward-shaping tool for training a novel dexterous gripper across multiple machines.
