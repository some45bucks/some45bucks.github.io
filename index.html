Selected work

Heterogeneous multi-agent learning in Isaac Lab

IROS 2025, first author

Brought heterogeneous-agent RL algorithms into NVIDIA Isaac Lab so teams of different robots can learn to work together in GPU-parallel physics simulation.

Master's thesis: one robot, many agents

M.S. thesis, Utah State University, 2025

Split a Spot quadruped into cooperating agents, one per leg or joint group, and showed that HAPPO's sequential updates learn a better-coordinated gait at almost no cost in training speed.
